Vegetable Salad

Preparation: 10 Minutes | Easy

A fresh and healthy salad made with colourful vegetables.

Ingredients

  • Cucumber
  • Tomato
  • Carrot
  • Lettuce
  • Lemon Juice

Preparation

Wash and chop all vegetables. Mix them in a bowl, add lemon juice and salt, then serve fresh.

Homemade Pizza

Preparation: 30 Minutes | Medium

A delicious pizza topped with cheese and vegetables.

Ingredients

  • Pizza Base
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Mozzarella Cheese
  • Onion
  • Capsicum

Preparation

Spread sauce over the pizza base. Add vegetables and cheese. Bake until the cheese melts and the base becomes crispy.

Veg Noodles

Preparation: 20 Minutes | Easy

Spicy and tasty noodles mixed with fresh vegetables.

Ingredients

  • Noodles
  • Carrot
  • Cabbage
  • Capsicum
  • Soy Sauce

Preparation

Boil the noodles. Stir-fry vegetables, add sauces and mix with the cooked noodles.

Masala Dosa

Preparation: 25 Minutes | Medium

Crispy South Indian dosa filled with delicious potato masala.

Ingredients

  • Dosa Batter
  • Potatoes
  • Onion
  • Mustard Seeds
  • Curry Leaves

Preparation

Prepare potato masala using boiled potatoes and spices. Spread dosa batter on a hot pan, cook until crispy and add the masala filling.

White Sauce Pasta

Preparation: 25 Minutes | Medium

Creamy pasta prepared with white sauce and vegetables.

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Milk
  • Butter
  • Flour
  • Cheese

Preparation

Cook pasta. Prepare white sauce using butter, flour and milk. Add cheese and mix the pasta with the sauce.

Veg Fried Rice

Preparation: 20 Minutes | Easy

Delicious fried rice prepared with mixed vegetables.

Ingredients

  • Cooked Rice
  • Carrot
  • Beans
  • Spring Onion
  • Soy Sauce

Preparation

Stir-fry the vegetables, add cooked rice and sauces. Mix everything well and serve hot.
