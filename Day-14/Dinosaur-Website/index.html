Introduction to Dinosaurs

Dinosaurs were fascinating reptiles that lived on Earth millions of years ago. They first appeared during the Triassic Period, around 230 million years ago. They lived in different environments such as forests, deserts and plains. Dinosaurs came in many shapes and sizes. Some were enormous plant-eaters, while others were powerful meat-eaters. They walked on two or four legs, and some had long necks, sharp teeth, horns or heavy tails. Most non-bird dinosaurs became extinct about 66 million years ago. Today, scientists study dinosaur fossils to understand how these amazing creatures lived.

What Did Dinosaurs Eat?

Dinosaurs had different food habits depending on their body structure and the type of teeth they possessed.

  • Herbivores: Ate plants, leaves, fruits and vegetation.
  • Carnivores: Ate other animals and meat.
  • Omnivores: Ate both plants and animals.

Where Did Dinosaurs Live?

Dinosaur fossils have been discovered on every continent, including Antarctica. They lived in different environments that provided food, water and shelter.

Interesting Facts About Dinosaurs

  • Dinosaurs lived millions of years before humans.
  • Some dinosaurs were smaller than chickens.
  • Not all dinosaurs were meat-eaters.
  • Birds are the living descendants of theropod dinosaurs.
  • Scientists study fossils to understand dinosaurs.
